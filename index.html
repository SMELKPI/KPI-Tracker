How Points Are Calculated

Your monthly performance score is the sum of all KPI category points. Each category has a defined monthly target and a maximum point value. Your incentive payout depends on the tier your final rating falls into at the month-end.

Employee Rating (ESAU Model)

Monthly performance of every SMEL targeted employee is rated by the ESAU model:

Supervisor KPI and Target

Supervisor will have a target consisting of all the targets of RE & RO under their supervision. Minimum headcount requirement under each BDM/SBDM is 15 and Acting BDM is 12. However, if anyone achieves the full target without the minimum headcount, they will be eligible for commission. In that case, the missing headcount target will be considered of RE, Rural area.

Supervisor KPI Criteria

CriteriaParameters
RO/RE combined target (% on ESAU basis)Combined Point target for all RO/RE under supervision as per job grade and branch category.
RO/RE individual rating achievedIf 50% of the RO/RE gets under S grade, supervisor's KPI rating is lowered by one grade for incentive calculation only. ESAU rating remains the same.
